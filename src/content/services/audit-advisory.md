## 1. Statutory and regulatory audit

- **Financial statement audit:** Independent verification of accounts to ensure accuracy and compliance.
- **Tax audit:** Checking compliance with tax laws and reporting obligations.
- **Regulatory audit:** Industry-specific audits (banks, insurance, NGOs, etc.).

## 2. Internal audit and risk management

- **Process review:** Evaluating internal controls, workflows and efficiency.
- **Risk assessment:** Identifying financial, operational and compliance risks.
- **Fraud detection:** Spotting irregularities and strengthening safeguards.

## 3. Advisory services

- **Business strategy consulting:** Guidance on growth, restructuring and expansion.
- **Financial advisory:** Capital structuring, investment planning and cost optimization.
- **Corporate governance:** Setting up boards, policies and compliance frameworks.

## 4. Transaction and special advisory

- **Due diligence:** Financial and legal checks before mergers, acquisitions or investments.
- **Valuation services:** Determining fair value of businesses or assets.
- **IPO and capital market advisory:** Preparing companies for listing and investor readiness.

## 5. Legal and compliance support

- **Regulatory filings:** Ensuring timely submission to authorities.
- **Contract review:** Checking agreements for compliance and risk.
- **Dispute resolution:** Advisory on tax, corporate or financial disputes.

Protects businesses from legal pitfalls.
