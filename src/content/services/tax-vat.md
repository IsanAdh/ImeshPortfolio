## 1. Compliance services (the essentials)

These are the routine filings and registrations every business must do:

- **Income tax returns:** For individuals, partnerships and companies.
- **VAT registration and filing:** Monthly or trimester VAT returns, depending on turnover.
- **TDS (tax deducted at source):** Deduction, deposit and filing of TDS returns.
- **Excise and other levies:** Where applicable, ensuring proper reporting.
- **IRD correspondence:** Responding to notices, assessments and clarifications.

## 2. Advisory and planning (the strategic side)

Here the CA acts as a tax strategist:

- **Tax planning:** Structuring transactions to minimize tax legally.
- **VAT optimization:** Advising on input VAT credits, refunds and exemptions.
- **Cross-border taxation:** Guidance on imports, exports and double taxation treaties.
- **Business structuring:** Choosing between proprietorship, partnership or company for tax efficiency.

## 3. Representation and legal support

When disputes or audits arise:

- **Tax audit support:** Preparing audit reports and defending positions.
- **Appeals and litigation:** Representing clients before IRD, Revenue Tribunal or courts.
- **Penalty and interest negotiation:** Handling disputes over late filings or misstatements.
- **Advance rulings:** Seeking official clarifications on complex tax matters.

## 4. Value-added services

Beyond compliance, a CA can offer:

- **Digital tax dashboards:** Real-time tracking of VAT, TDS and filings.
- **Training and workshops:** Educating staff on tax compliance.
- **Due diligence:** Tax health checks before mergers or investments.
- **Automation:** Setting up accounting software for auto-filing VAT/TDS.
