## 1. Business valuation

- **Purpose:** Determining the fair market value of the startup.
- **Methods:** Discounted cash flow, comparable company analysis, asset-based valuation.
- **Outcome:** A credible valuation report that investors trust.

## 2. Financial models and projections

- **Customized models:** Revenue forecasts, expense planning, cash flow analysis.
- **Scenario mapping:** Best-case, worst-case and realistic projections.
- **Investor-ready outputs:** Clear dashboards and Excel models for presentations.

## 3. Investor-ready checklist and due diligence

- **Compliance review:** Ensuring tax, legal and regulatory filings are in order.
- **Documentation support:** Preparing cap tables, contracts and governance records.
- **Due diligence prep:** Anticipating investor questions and audits.

## 4. Virtual CFO services

- **Strategic guidance:** Acting as a part-time CFO for early-stage companies.
- **Fundraising support:** Assisting with pitch decks, negotiations and term sheets.
- **Financial discipline:** Setting up reporting systems and controls.
