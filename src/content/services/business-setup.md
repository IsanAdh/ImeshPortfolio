## 1. Choosing the right business structure

- **Options:** Proprietorship, partnership, private limited, public limited, NGO/INGO, cooperative.
- **Guidance:** Advising on tax efficiency, liability protection and compliance needs.

## 2. Company registration (OCR)

- **Process:** Name approval, drafting the Memorandum and Articles of Association, submission to the Office of Company Registrar (OCR).
- **Documents needed:** Citizenship copies, proposed MOA/AOA, address proof, capital details.
- **Timeline:** Usually 5–7 working days if documents are complete.

## 3. PAN and VAT registration

- **PAN (Permanent Account Number):** Mandatory for all businesses to transact legally.
- **VAT registration:** Required if annual turnover exceeds the threshold or if the business deals in VAT-liable goods or services.
- **Timeline:** 2–3 working days after company registration.

## 4. Annual compliance and filings

- **Annual returns:** Filing with OCR and IRD.
- **Board resolutions and minutes:** Maintaining governance records.
- **Renewals:** Updating licenses, permits and registrations.

## 5. Legal and advisory support

- **Drafting agreements:** Shareholder agreements, partnership deeds, employment contracts.
- **Regulatory guidance:** Industry-specific compliance (banks, NGOs, manufacturing, etc.).
- **Dispute resolution:** Handling legal notices or compliance disputes.
