## What we collect

This website has no login and does not store form submissions. Contact and booking forms open WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, so the message is sent only when you press send in WhatsApp.

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is processed by our email provider.

## Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, in aggregate.

## Your choices

To have your information removed, contact us through the details on the Contact page.
