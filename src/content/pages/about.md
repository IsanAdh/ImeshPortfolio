## My story

Hi, I'm Imesh.

By training, I'm a Chartered Accountant.<br />
By habit, I'm someone who can't stop asking, "What if this could be better?"

My days start with numbers. At Imesh & Associates, Chartered Accountants, I help businesses, from small firms to growing companies, keep their books clean, their taxes right and their decisions clear. I believe good accounting isn't about paperwork. It's about giving people the confidence to grow.

But numbers also showed me something else: what we throw away has value too.

That idea became Re-Nepal. We collect waste that doesn't break down on its own and turn it into useful products, one step towards cleaner streets and a more circular Nepal.

Accounting taught me discipline. Sustainability gave me purpose. Today I try to bring both together, building businesses that make sense on paper and for the planet.

If you're a business owner looking for a trusted CA, or someone who simply cares about Nepal's future, I'd love to hear from you.

**CA Imesh Adhikari**<br />
